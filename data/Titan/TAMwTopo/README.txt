10 Year TAM Global Simulation (from J. Lora, 1/11/2024, 2/9/2026) with Topography

6-Hourly Winds interpolated to 10-m level from surface

64 points per Titan day

Starts in northern autumnal equinox.


lon = longitude 
lat = latitude
ucomp = zonal component of wind (positive values = Eastward wind)
vcomp = meridinonal component of wind (positive values = Northward wind)
time = time (every 6 hours)


Model time step of 10 minutes, averaged to 6 hours

Atmosphere of 24 layers with bottom layer determined by pressure/terrain and extrapolated to 10-m elevation from surface

Simulations continued from runs in Lora+2022 after 150 Titan year spinup to a statistical steady state for atmospheric temperature and winds. The precipitation and hydrology can continue to drift but winds generally spin up quickly

Used topography from Corlies et al. 2017 (as in Lora+2022)

Includes full hydrology model from Faulk+2020 (descrived in Lora+2022)

Constant albedo (0.2) and surface roughness (0.005 m) as in Lora+2015

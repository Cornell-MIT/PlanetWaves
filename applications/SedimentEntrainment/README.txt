Scripts for sediment entrainment under tides and waves


(1) Tidal_Flows : predictions for normalized shear from tidal flows in straits and nearshore on Shields diagram
(2) Wave_Flow   : predictions for depth of entrainment under waves on Titan (bedload)
			- relies on wave model for deepwater wave properties from Titan_DeepwaterWaves.m
			  where past runs of this script are stored in past_runs/Titan_DeepwaterWaves.mat
